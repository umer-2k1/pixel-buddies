## Intent

Launch teaser for **Buddies**, a desktop app: a cute animated companion (panda, cat,
bunny, penguin, axolotl) that walks onto your screen, holds a water bottle, and asks
"Have you had some water?" — then celebrates or encourages. Exciting and joyful, fast cuts
synced to an upbeat track with punchy sound effects. Energetic but never childish; polished.

## Assets

- ../../src/shared/mascot.js — the real character renderer (all five buddies, all expressions).
- ../../build/icon.png — the app icon.

## Customizations

- No voice-over: kinetic on-screen text, music bed + SFX only (pop, whoosh, sparkle, glug, chime).
- Characters are the real in-app artwork, posed with the app's own expressions.

## Notes

- No website to capture (no-capture path). Product facts come from the app itself:
  five buddies, walks in and asks with Yes / Not yet buttons, reacts with emotion, mini-games,
  reminders on your schedule, runs quietly from the menu bar, macOS (Windows/Linux builds possible).
- Do not invent metrics, testimonials, or pricing. CTA is simply "Buddies — your desktop water buddies."
