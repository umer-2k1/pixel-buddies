Phone alarms get swiped away. So Buddies taps you on the shoulder — gently, and with a smile.

What it does

A reminder with personality.

🚶

Walks onto your screen

Your buddy strolls in holding a water bottle and asks if you’ve had a drink. No boring pop-ups, then it wanders off again.

😄

Reacts to your answer

Say Yes for confetti and a shared sip. Say Not yet and get a gentle, guilt-free nudge — never nagging.

🐼

Five buddies to pick from

Bao the panda, Mochi the cat, Pip the bunny, Pebble the penguin and Ollie the axolotl. Switch any time.

⏰

Your schedule, your rules

Every few minutes or at set times, with active hours, snooze and pause. It always tells you when it’s coming back.

🎮

Little games for breaks

Drop Catch, Memory Match and Perfect Pour — plus a daily goal and streaks to keep you going.

🔒

Quiet & private

Lives in your menu bar and uses tiny synthesized sounds you can mute. Everything stays on your Mac: no account, no tracking.

Mac, fastest way: paste this into Terminal. It picks the right version, checks it, installs it and opens it.

curl -fsSL https://umer-2k1.github.io/pixel-buddies/install.sh | bash
First time opening it? (one-time step)

Mac: Buddies isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once. If it says it can’t be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. The Terminal command above skips this step.

Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

Requires macOS 10.15 or later, or Windows 10/11. Watch the repository on GitHub to be first to know when it’s out.
