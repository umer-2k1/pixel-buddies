## The story (why it matters)

**Who it's for:** anyone who works in long stretches. Developers, designers, writers, students, anyone with "just one more email" energy.

**The problem:** you sit down at 9 AM. At 3 PM you realise you haven't had a sip. Headaches, tiredness, a foggy afternoon. Water apps send a notification, and a notification is the easiest thing in the world to swipe away.

**What it saves you:** the 3 PM crash. A glass of water takes ten seconds. Forgetting it costs you an afternoon.

**Why a character:** we ignore alerts but we don't ignore a friend. A little panda walks in holding a bottle, looks at you and asks. Saying "not yet" feels rude. Saying "yes" gets a celebration. It turns a chore into a tiny moment you look forward to.

**The outcome:** more water, steadier energy, and a streak you actually want to keep.

## Maker's first comment
Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm [your name], and I built Buddies.

I work in long stretches. I'd sit down at 9, look up at 3, and realise I hadn't had a single glass of water. I tried reminder apps. Every one of them was a notification, and I swiped every one away.

What I didn't swipe away was a friend asking "hey, have you had some water?" So I built that. A tiny animated buddy (a panda, cat, bunny, penguin or axolotl) walks onto your screen with a water bottle and asks. Say yes and it celebrates. Say not yet and it says "no rush" and comes back later. It never guilt-trips.

What's inside:
- 5 characters you can switch between any time
- A daily goal, streaks and a weekly view
- 3 little mini-games for a break
- Soft synthesized sounds, mutable
- Free, open source (MIT), no account, no tracking

Honest note: it's a toy project made for fun, and it isn't code-signed yet (I don't have a developer account). On first launch, macOS may ask you to allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security, and Windows may show SmartScreen ("More info → Run anyway"). The Mac one-liner on the site avoids the prompt.

## Replies to likely questions
- **Why isn't it signed?** I'm a solo maker without a paid developer account yet. The code is open and the releases have SHA-256 checksums.
- **Is there a web or phone version?** No, it's a desktop-only app by design: a buddy that lives on your screen.
- **Does it collect data?** No. Everything stays on your machine.
- **Linux?** Not yet. Open an issue if you'd use it.
- **Can I add my own character?** The characters are SVG rigs in `src/shared/mascot.js`. PRs welcome.
